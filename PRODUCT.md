# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary visitor: a founder, operator, or partner who opens the public site to understand what Callisto is and whether it is worth writing. Their job is to grasp the stance in a few seconds, then look at selected work, notes, or send an email.

## Product Purpose

Callisto builds and operates software, services, and internet businesses. The website is a quiet public front door: a mark, a name, one sentence, then work, notes, and a way to write. Success is that a visitor knows who this is, can scan selected work and notes, and can email the team without a form funnel.

## Positioning

Callisto is an operator studio, not a single-product SaaS company and not an agency pitch. It builds software and then runs the businesses those systems support.

## Operating Context

The public site is a Next.js App Router project. The previous marketing site (product pages, book-demo, coming-soon pause) is evidence of history, not the public experience going forward. Public IA is home, work, notes, and contact-as-email.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Public navigation is lowercase: work, notes, contact.
- Contact is `mailto:contact@aicallisto.com`. No contact form is required.
- Work is selected work: industries, projects, or software Callisto built. Entries may be hardcoded.
- Notes is a simple index of writing. Structure should make later hardcoded entries easy.
- Synthetic placeholder work and notes are allowed for layout, and must be labeled so they cannot be mistaken for live claims.
- Do not invent customers, metrics, launch dates, or awards.
- Confirmed visitor copy: “callisto” and “we build and operate software, services and internet businesses.”
- Existing inner product pages (Cortex, Steward, industries) may be reused as work entries; they are not the site chassis.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Callisto, set in lowercase on the site.
- Contact: contact@aicallisto.com
- Existing marks: `/logo-removebg-preview.png` (light), `/IMG_0075.PNG` (dark), and the current crescent-in-disc mark. The public mark is a modest living orb (thinking-orbs at 64px), not a hero-scale moon.
- Palette constraint: grey / white / black, including gradients. No chromatic brand color required. Ground is dark, not paper-white.
- Type constraint: lowercase, modest sizes, not display-scale headlines.
- Voice: direct, small, operator-like. Not closed, not promotional.
- Reference the user made binding: the quiet density and spacing of hf0.com, without copying its glass card or apply funnel.
- Standing visual preference: the category-standard quiet studio colophon, played straight on a dark field. Small lowercase type, lots of air, a modest mark, three links. Craft bar: hf0.com.

## Evidence on Hand

- Logo assets in `public/` and the attached crescent-in-disc mark
- Prior product copy for Cortex, Steward, and related software in `src/app/software`
- No confirmed client list, metrics, or launch dates. Future work must not fabricate them.
- Placeholder work and notes will be authored as synthetic until replaced.

## Product Principles

- Be findable in one glance: mark, name, one sentence.
- Stay reachable: contact is an email, not a maze.
- Show work as a list of things operated, not a SaaS feature grid.
- Tell the truth: placeholders are labeled; claims stay uninventable.
- Keep the site small enough to update by hand.
