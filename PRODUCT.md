## Platform

web

## Users

Primary visitor during this pause: anyone who opens the live Callisto site (prospects, partners, returning contacts). Their job is to understand that a new site is on the way and to reach the team if they need something now.

## Product Purpose

Callisto builds AI-powered business solutions: custom platforms, workflow automation, analytics, and tools such as Cortex and Steward. The live website is the public front door. Success for this pause is that every public URL presents a branded holding screen, and that a visitor who needs Callisto can still email the team.

## Positioning

Callisto designs and deploys custom AI systems around a client’s actual operations, rather than selling a single off-the-shelf chatbot.

## Operating Context

The public site is a Next.js marketing site. During this pause the entire public surface is a coming-soon screen. Existing inner pages (products, contact, book-demo, software) remain in the repo but are not the public experience until the pause is lifted.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Whole public site shows the coming-soon screen, not only the homepage.
- Visitors must be able to email `gaspar@aicallisto.com`.
- Confirmed visitor copy: “A new Callisto is on the way. Reach us anytime.”
- The existing site (header, footer, product pages) stays in the codebase so it can return when the pause is lifted.
- Do not invent customers, metrics, launch dates, or capabilities beyond what the current site already states.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Callisto
- Contact: gaspar@aicallisto.com
- Existing marks: `/logo-removebg-preview.png` (light), `/IMG_0075.PNG` (dark)
- Voice for this pause: direct and reachable, not closed-for-business

## Evidence on Hand

- Current marketing site copy and product descriptions in `src/app` and `src/components`
- Logo assets in `public/`
- No confirmed launch date. Future work must not fabricate one.

## Product Principles

- Stay reachable: a pause is not a dead end.
- Tell the truth: a new site is coming; no fake dates or social proof.
- Keep the old site intact so the pause is reversible.
- Brand the wait as Callisto, not as generic maintenance.
